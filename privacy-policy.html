2Information we do not collect

Orvixa does not collect, store on our servers, sell, rent or share any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the app:

  • does not ask you to create an account or log in;
  • does not access your contacts, microphone, camera, location, call logs or SMS;
  • does not use advertising IDs, third-party advertising networks, or analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • does not read your photos or files. It only saves wallpapers you choose to download.

3How the app works

All wallpapers shown in Orvixa come from our own curated collection, managed by us. When you open or refresh the app, it connects to our servers and cloud hosting to fetch the latest list of wallpapers (image links, titles, categories, owner names, dates, AI-generated and license information). The wallpaper images are then loaded from the links in that list.

Like any internet request, the servers that host the collection and images may receive standard technical data such as your IP address, device type and the time of the request. Orvixa itself does not collect or store this data.

6Saving, sharing and feedback

  • Save: copies the wallpaper file to Pictures/Orvixa on your device.
  • Share: opens the Android share sheet with a link to the wallpaper and the app. What happens next depends on the app you choose.
  • Send Feedback / Report: opens your email app with a prefilled message. Feedback emails may include your device manufacturer, model and Android version, which you can edit or delete before sending. We only receive what you choose to send.

7Content, AI-generated images and copyright

Some wallpapers in Orvixa may be created with the help of AI tools. Where we know this, the wallpaper's Info screen shows "AI generated". The Info screen also shows the owner, upload and update dates, and copyright or license details when available.

We try to make sure every wallpaper is original, licensed or free to use. Wallpapers are provided for personal use as a phone wallpaper. You may not resell or redistribute them as your own work unless the license shown in the Info screen allows it.

Copyright owner, or think a wallpaper is inappropriate? Tap Report on the Info screen or email us with a link to the wallpaper and a short description. We review reports and remove content promptly where appropriate.

8Children's privacy

Orvixa does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under the applicable age in your country). The app has no ads, social features or user-generated content. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9Security

We use encrypted (HTTPS) connections for the wallpaper collection. Because we do not collect personal information, there is no user database that could be exposed. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Third-party services

Orvixa itself includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. The only third parties involved are the hosting and infrastructure providers that serve the collection and images, and the apps you choose to use for sharing or email. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

11Your rights and choices

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control everything stored by the app through the options in section 4. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights relating to your personal data. If you have a question or request, contact us and we will respond.

12Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how information is handled (for example ads, analytics or accounts), we will update this policy before releasing them and change the "Last updated" date above. The latest version is always available at the link in the Google Play listing and inside the app.
